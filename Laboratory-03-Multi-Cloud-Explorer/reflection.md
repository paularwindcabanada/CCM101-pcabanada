# Mission 3 Reflection
This laboratory activity helped me understand cloud computing in a more practical way. By comparing AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, I learned that although they offer many of the same services, each one has its own strengths. Before this activity, I only knew them as popular cloud providers, but I did not really understand what made them different.

Among the three, AWS impressed me the most because of the wide variety of services it offers. I also learned that Azure can be a good option for businesses that already use Microsoft products and services. Google Cloud, on the other hand, is known for its strong features in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data, and Kubernetes.

One thing I noticed is that AWS, Azure, and GCP have an important similarity: they allow businesses to use computing resources without having to buy and maintain all the physical hardware themselves. They also provide services for storage, networking, security, databases, and application development.

I realized that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about picking the most popular one. A business needs to consider its budget, existing systems, security requirements, application needs, location, and future growth. The best choice depends on what the organization actually needs.

Being a Cloud Solutions Consultant for this activity also helped me see technology from a business perspective. I learned that a good solution should match the client's needs instead of choosing a platform just because it is well-known.

Lastly, working on my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio helped me become more comfortable with documenting my work. Adding the research, comparison tables, recommendations, screenshots, and reflection made my portfolio more organized. Overall, this activity gave me a better understanding of cloud computing and made me more confident in using GitHub and Markdown for my school activities.
